01What to show

What a construction company website must show

Clients usually look for a builder on their phone, between other things. In the first seconds they need to understand three things: what you build, where you work and whether the company is real. That is why every site I make for builders has these blocks.

  1. 01

    Services in plain words

    Not just “construction works”, but specifically: private houses, roofs, facades, interior finishing, renovation. Each service with a short description and a photo, so the client sees it is your work.

  2. 02

    Projects with photos and places

    Finished jobs with the town, the year and a short description. Real photos of your projects convince more than any text. If you have video, it can go in too, as on the V & M Būve site.

  3. 03

    Registrations and trust

    Your company number, the construction register entry (BIS in Latvia, MTR in Estonia), certificates and years in business. Larger clients check this first, so it is better to show it yourself.

  4. 04

    Service area

    The towns and regions where you work. A visitor sees at once whether you will come to them, and search engines see where you operate.

  5. 05

    One-tap contact

    Phone, WhatsApp and e-mail that open with one tap, plus a link to the map. A client can send photos of the site and a question straight away.

  6. 06

    Languages

    Your main language first, Russian or English where needed. The CONSTRUCTA site runs in Latvian and Russian.

  7. 07

    Fast and easy on a phone

    The page loads quickly even on weak mobile internet at a building site, text is readable without zooming, buttons are finger-sized.

05Questions

Common questions

01How much does a construction company website cost?

From 300 € one-time for the whole site, up to 5 pages: home, services, projects with photos, contacts and a privacy policy. There is no monthly fee. The domain costs about 10 to 15 € a year, which you pay to the registrar yourself. In Poland the price is from 1 990 zł.

02I don’t have good photos. What now?

I start with what is already public: your Facebook page, listings, your old site. Phone photos of your projects work if they are sharp and bright. I never show stock photos as your work.

03Can I show my registrations and certificates?

Yes, and I recommend it. Company number, register entries, certificates and company details usually sit in the footer or on the About page.

04How long does it take?

I send the first version of your homepage within 24 hours. Then I prepare the whole site while you review it and send corrections. When everything is right, I put the site live on your domain.

05Will the site be visible on Google?

Every site is prepared for search engines: titles, descriptions, a sitemap and structured data about the company. Nobody can guarantee positions on Google, but a well-built site plus a Google Business Profile is the best start.

06Who changes texts and photos after launch?

For a month after launch, edits are free: send a new text or photo and I put it in. If you want to change things yourself, a simple admin panel can be added.
