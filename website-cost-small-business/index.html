What costs extra

What costs extra

  • Domain: about 10 to 15 € a year. You pay it to the registrar yourself, and it is registered in your company’s name.
  • E-mail on your domain (for example info@your-firm.lv): 50 €.
  • Logo: 150 €.
  • More than 5 pages, a second language or an admin panel for editing: I quote the price before any work.

Why there is no monthly fee

The site is static: it needs no paid server, no database and no plugin updates. That is why hosting is 0 € and there is nothing to maintain every month.

What to ask any web designer

  • Is there a monthly or yearly fee, and what for?
  • In whose name is the domain registered?
  • Will I get all the files and the source code?
  • Will I see the design before I pay?
  • Who puts in new texts and photos, and how fast?

05Questions

Common questions

01Is there a monthly or yearly fee?

No. The site costs 300 € one-time and hosting is 0 €. The only yearly payment is the domain, about 10 to 15 € a year, to the registrar.

02When do I pay?

Once, when the site is ready and you like it. I send the invoice after your “yes”. If you don’t like the first version, you pay nothing.

03How can I pay?

By bank transfer against an invoice. Paying by card through a payment link is also possible.

04Why so cheap compared with agencies?

I work alone, without an office or a sales team, and the sites are static: no paid server and no plugins to maintain. I show the first version before any payment, so you see the quality before you decide.

05Who owns the site and the domain?

You do. The domain is registered in your company’s name, and after payment you get all the files and the source code.

06What if I need more pages later?

Write what you need and I will quote the price before any work. I never add or charge anything without your agreement.
