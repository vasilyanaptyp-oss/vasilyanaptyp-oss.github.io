How it works

You risk nothing until you have seen the page.

Four steps, usually inside one week. No calls needed: everything happens in writing, in your language.

  1. 01

    I find you

    Usually in a business directory, with a phone number and a Facebook page but no site, or a site from 2014 that breaks on a phone.

  2. 02

    I write, in your language

    One short email naming one concrete thing: photos that nobody sees, a phone number you cannot tap, a homepage that does not open over https.

  3. 03

    You get a concept, free

    Built from your own photos, services and contacts. On a live link, so you can open it on your phone and show your family.

  4. 04

    You say yes, or no

    Yes means 300 € for the full site, all files and code, and a month of free edits. No means we part as friends and the link stays up.
